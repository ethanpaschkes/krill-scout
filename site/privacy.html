Privacy policy

Krill Scout, a free Chrome extension for the Krillion trivia game. Last updated 22 November 2026.

The short version

Krill Scout collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no ads.

What it reads

  • The prompt on Krillion game pages, so it can suggest answers.
  • The game's reply to answers you submit (the tier you got), so it can learn which picks score well.

It runs only on krillion.io and playdaily.org/krillion. It cannot see any other website.

What it stores

Your settings and a list of up to 2,000 of your own past answers and the tiers they got. These stay in Chrome's storage on your computer and are never uploaded. "Forget what it learned" in the 🦐 menu deletes them, and removing the extension deletes everything.

AI

For some prompts it uses Chrome's built-in AI (Gemini Nano), which runs on your own computer. Prompts are not sent to any AI service.
