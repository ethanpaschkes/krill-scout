What it does

Trained on 128 past dives

Its rarity model learned from the real tiers of 896 past daily prompts and 185,000 answers. On dives it never saw, it guesses the right tier twice as often as word counts alone.

Word prompts, instantly

"Containing ough", "ending in -ism", "five letters starting with Q": it searches a real dictionary and picks rare words that fit, in under a tenth of a second.

9,420 rare answers, instantly

A built-in bank covers 361 common topics, from car brands to Beatles songs. Tested on past prompts, 87% of its picks scored Deep Cut or better. Anything else goes to Chrome's free built-in AI, fact-checked.

Learns from your games

Every answer you submit shows the tier you really got. Krill Scout learns from that, so its tier guesses fit Krillion better the more you play.

10 picks, one keypress

The top pick lands in the answer box. Press 1-9 or 0 to swap in any of the 10. You decide, you press DIVE.

Nothing leaves your computer

No key, no account, no server. The model, the answer bank and Chrome's AI all run on your machine.

Install in 2 minutes

  1. Download krill-scout.zip and double-click it to unzip.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped krill-scout folder.
  4. Click the 🦐 icon in the toolbar. If it says Chrome AI needs a download, click Set up Chrome AI (free, one time).
  5. Open krillion.io and start a dive. Picks show up in the top-right corner.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store for one-click install. The code is open source on GitHub.
